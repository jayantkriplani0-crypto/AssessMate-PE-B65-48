# Milestone 1 Sample Input & Expected Output (Baseline Reference)

## Sample Input
- **Syllabus:** "Photosynthesis is the process by which green plants use light energy to make glucose from carbon dioxide and water, releasing oxygen."
- **Topic:** Photosynthesis
- **Bloom's level:** Remember
- **Difficulty:** Easy
- **Type:** Short answer
- **Quantity:** 1
- **Marks:** 2

## Sample Expected Output
- **Q1.** What is photosynthesis? (2 marks)
- **Answer:** Photosynthesis is the process by which green plants use light energy to make glucose from carbon dioxide and water, releasing oxygen.
- **Marking:** 1 mark for identifying it as the process plants use to make food; 1 mark for mentioning light energy and the raw materials/products.
- **Review:** Clear, answerable from the supplied text, and suited to Remember level. Teacher verifies the answer and marking scheme before use.
